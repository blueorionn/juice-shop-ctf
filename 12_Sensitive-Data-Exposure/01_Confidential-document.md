# Confidential Document

## Objective

Access a confidential document.

## Solution

After a little bit of reconnaissance, `/ftp` path was found in the `robots.txt` file. The `/ftp` path leads to a bunch of files, one of them being **`acquisitions.md`**, which is our required confidential file.
